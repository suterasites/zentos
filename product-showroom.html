Every finish for your home, in one place.

The experience

Two levels of inspiration.

Fitting out your home should be an exciting process, so we give you everything you need to let your imagination run free. You will be captivated by our huge range of interior and exterior design options, and inspired by an extensive selection of colours, materials, fittings, floorings and appliances.

Things look different on paper, so we want you to actually touch and interact with the range before you decide. Our full-sized room displays, including three bathrooms and three kitchens, help you picture your décor in a true-to-life setting.

A one-stop-shop

A complete product selection centre, not a simple display home.

Our range covers just about everything you can think of, so you can fit out the whole house in one visit with no need to go anywhere else. Choose with confidence, knowing how it all looks and feels together.

Kitchens & cabinetry Benchtops Tapware Baths & showers Toilets & vanities Tiles Flooring Ovens & appliances Lighting Blinds Windows Cladding Bricks Wardrobes Staircases Security doors Garage doors Security systems Solar & heating And plenty more

Why the showroom matters

Guided, considered choices, made simple.

Quality and value

We cater for every budget, but only stock reputable brands from suppliers we know and trust. Our supplier network gives us the buying power of much larger construction companies, which we pass on in your final price.

Colour consultancy

Feeling overwhelmed, or just after some advice? Our specialist colour consultant can help you create a scheme that works together, from the floor to the façade. We highly recommend it.

Obligation-free visits

Building a new home is a big move, so you are welcome to visit even before you have decided to build with us. Visits are by appointment, so get in touch to book a time.
