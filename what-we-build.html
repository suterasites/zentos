What We Build

Boutique residential builds, done four ways.

Zentos has been building across Melbourne since 2002. We keep our numbers small so our standards stay high, and we focus on four kinds of residential work we know well. Whatever the site or the brief, the approach is the same: stay close to the detail, protect the design, and hand over a build that was worth the wait.

Multi-Unit Developments Medium Density Builds Sloping Block Builds Architectural Builds

01 / Multi-Unit

Multi-Unit Developments

Townhouses and multi-dwelling developments delivered end to end. We work with developers and owners to balance yield, buildability and a finish that protects long-term value, from early planning through to handover.

  • Dual-occupancy, townhouse and multi-dwelling projects
  • Feasibility and buildability input early, before drawings are locked
  • A clear scope and a single point of contact through construction
  • Finishes specified to hold their value at resale or lease
Read more about multi-unit developments

02 / Medium Density

Medium Density Builds

Low-rise apartment and townhouse projects that lift density without losing a considered, residential feel. We build to the amenity, the streetscape and the numbers, so the result works for residents and for the project.

  • Walk-up apartments and larger townhouse clusters
  • Planning-compliant layouts that keep amenity front of mind
  • Shared services and common areas built for low maintenance
  • A streetscape that reads residential, not institutional
Read more about medium density builds

03 / Sloping Block

Sloping Block Builds

Difficult and steep sites are where we do our best work. We turn challenging blocks into homes that make the most of the fall, the outlook and the light, with the engineering handled properly from the ground up.

  • Split-level and step-down designs that follow the land
  • Retaining, drainage and footings engineered for the site
  • Outlook and natural light planned around the slope
  • Access and staging worked out before we break ground
Read more about sloping block builds

04 / Architectural

Architectural Builds

Considered, custom homes built alongside your architect and designer. We protect the detail rather than cut corners, and we stay close to the drawings so the home that gets built is the home that was designed.

  • Close collaboration with your architect and consultants
  • Careful detailing and finishing across the whole build
  • Transparent communication through every stage
  • A build managed to protect the design intent
Read more about architectural builds
