Multi-Unit Developments

Units, townhouses and dual-occupancy homes are the new normal across Melbourne's suburbs, and a real opportunity for astute owners and investors to capitalise on rising land values. If you own a block with development potential, we can guide the whole project in a coordinated way, from feasibility through to handover.

Overview

Multi-Unit Developments

Whether it is a large development or the subdivision of a family home, we have the experience to steer the process end to end. As with our custom homes, we take care of every aspect of the build, including design, permits and demolition. We are also experts at working with unconventional or sloping sites.

  • Dual-occupancy, townhouse and multi-dwelling projects
  • Feasibility and buildability input early, before drawings are locked
  • Design, permits and demolition handled under one roof
  • A clear scope and a single point of contact through construction
