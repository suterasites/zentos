Who we are

A builder that stays close to the work.

Zentos is a founder-led building business with a proud history of construction excellence and customer satisfaction. Servicing Melbourne suburbs since 2002, we have forged a strong reputation for quality, integrity and value.

We bring genuine passion and dedication to every project, and because we take on considered work rather than volume, that focus carries right through to the finished result. Whether it is a difficult sloping site, a multi-unit development or an architectural home, the same people stay close to the detail from the first site walk to handover.

Our Managing Director

Serkan Aslan

Founder & Managing Director

Serkan is the founder and Managing Director of Zentos. He understands the construction industry inside out, and brings over two decades of experience to every project. His trade and management skills built the business, but his real passion is helping people realise their dreams.

"Construction is more than just an occupation for me. It's a lifestyle and a passion."

A hands-on leader, Serkan is driven to take a vision and see it come to life. Clients value his creative flair, sound judgement and honest advice, all aimed at keeping projects on track and on budget.

"I translate what is on the paper and bring it to life. But I'm also realistic, and approach every project from the perspective of my clients."

The Zentos difference

What sets a Zentos build apart.

Eight things you can count on, from your first conversation through to the keys.

Expertise

We know construction and we do it well. We specialise in difficult and unconventional projects, and are experts at managing sloping sites. Every project gets our full attention, not a production line.

Personalised service

We are a boutique business and we care for our clients. From your first contact, you will find a personal touch in everything we do, with close dialogue at every step of the journey to your home. Our commercial clients get the same approach, built into long-term partnerships.

Control

Our relationship with you doesn't end when you sign a contract. We encourage you to take an active role, visit your site regularly, and liaise directly with your builder rather than a site supervisor. We put you in the driver's seat.

Flexibility

Every building project is unique, and building your home can be complex. We give you the flexibility to adapt your designs as the project evolves, without costs escalating out of control.

Value

We offer fixed-price contracts with no gimmicks and no hidden costs. We make smart decisions to keep costs down, including strong supplier relationships and a lean operation, and pass the savings on to you.

Quality

We take pride in our reputation and won't compromise on quality, in our materials, our workmanship or our customer service. We aim to exceed your expectations, so that you'll happily recommend Zentos.

Choice

We tailor inclusions to each home and budget, and offer a choice of quality products so your home gets the finish it deserves. Choose from our Standard or Zentos Deluxe range, or talk to us about a fully custom fit-out.

Sustainability

We only work with reputable suppliers who share a clear commitment to sustainability, so your home is built responsibly as well as beautifully.
